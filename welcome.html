You're on the dark side now.

Confluence Dark Mode is installed and already switched on. Open any Confluence page and it just works - here's what else it can do.

  • 🎨 Five themes, plus your own Classic, Dim, Deep, AMOLED and Warm - or drag the darkness, contrast and warmth sliders.
  • 🏢 Self-hosted Confluence too Instances on your own domain are detected automatically. Any other page can be added by hand from the popup.
  • 🕗 Always on, on a schedule, or in step with your system Set it once under Advanced and forget about it.
  • ⌨️ Toggle without leaving the page Press Alt Shift D - rebind it at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.
